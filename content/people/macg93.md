Irish poet and literary critic. After military service in WWI, MacGreevy earned a degree at Trinity College Dublin and worked as an art critic in Dublin. He met JJ in Paris in 1924. While living in Paris, he helped JJ correct proofs for transition magazine, contributed an essay to Our Exagmination Round His Factification for Incamination of Work in Progress (Paris: Shakespeare and Company, 1929), and contributed poetry and criticism to numerous literary magazines. Returning to Dublin, he published his Poems (London: Heinemann, 1934) and became Director of the National Gallery in 1950. **William Brockman**
