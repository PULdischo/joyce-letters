British poet, critic, and editor. Symons had been introduced to Joyce by [W. B. Yeats](/people/yeat65/), after which Symons corresponded with Joyce about his collection of poems, Chamber Music. In October 1906 Symons recommended the book to [Elkin Mathews](/people/math51/), who published it in May 1907. Symons and Joyce corresponded sporadically through the early 1930s, and Symons contributed the “Epilogue” to The Joyce Book, edited by [Herbert Hughes](/people/hugh82/) (London: Sylvan Press and Humphrey Milford Oxford University Press, 1933). **Dirk Van Hulle** and **William Brockman**
