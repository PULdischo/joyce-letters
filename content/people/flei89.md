American literary agent in Paris for Boni and Liveright. Fleischman met Joyce in Paris in late 1921; they corresponded sporadically for several years as Fleischman tried to interest Joyce in publishing Ulysses in the United States, but to no avail. He was married to [Helen Kastor](/people/joyc94/) (who later married [Giorgio Joyce](/people/joyc05/)) from 1915 to 1928 and was the father of [David Fleischman](/people/flei19/). **William Brockman**
