English poet, novelist, and, later, biographer. Assistant Editor of The Egoist. While giving [Ulysses](/works/W_ULYSSES/) a mixed review (“The Influence of Mr. James Joyce”, English Review 32 [April 1921], 333-41), he harshly dismissed Finnegans Wake as “pedantic nonsense” (“The Bookshelf”, Atlantic 163, no. 6 [June 1939]). **William Brockman**
