Michigan bookseller and literature professor at Stephens College, Missouri. He edited the literary magazineSignatures: Work in Progress (1936–38), and was himself a writer of fiction. In 1934, Thompson acquired the ‘Before Sunrise’ notebook, containing JJ’s translation of Vor Sonnenaufgang by [Gerhart Hauptmann](/people/haup62/). JJ, acting through [Paul Léon](/people/leon93/), refused Thompson permission to publish the translation, which in the 1970s was acquired by the Huntington Library and published as Joyce and Hauptmann: Before Sunrise: James Joyce's Translation, ed. Jill Perkins (San Marino: Huntington Library, 1978) **William Brockman**
