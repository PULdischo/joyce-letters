German and French poet. Born in Alsace-Lorraine, he met JJ during WWI in Zurich. He became a representative of the Rhein-Verlag in Paris and proposed a French translation of [A Portrait of the Artist as a Young Man](/works/W_APORTRAIT/), which failed to materialize. He participated in the translation of “Anna Livie Plurabelle” ([La Nouvelle revue française](/works/ID_MISSINg/), 1931) and published a French translation of “Ecce Puer” in 1933 along with several articles on JJ. As a poet he was loosely affiliated with Surrealism, collaborated with his wife [Claire Goll](/people/goll90/) (1891-1977) and founded the magazine [Surréalisme](/works/ID_MISSINg/) in October 1924. He spent the WWII years in New York and died in Paris. **William Brockman**
