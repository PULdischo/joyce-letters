Czech husband of JJ’s sister [Eileen](/people/joyc89/), who met him when, employed at the Živnostenská Banka in Trieste, he had been taking English lessons with JJ. They married in April 1915, spending several years in Prague before returning to Trieste. When JJ and his family returned to Trieste after their stay in Zurich, they lived for nine months in a crowded flat at Via della Sanità with Frantisek, Eileen, their two daughters and JJ’s brother [Stanislaus](/people/joyc84/). On 8 November 1926 Frantisek, having been suspected of embezzling bank funds, committed suicide while Eileen and their children were visiting Ireland. **William Brockman**
