Prominent Irish publisher of the literary revival years. Hone co-founded Maunsel & Company in 1905. The firm published a short-lived but influential literary magazine, _The Shanachie_, and through 1923 published some 500 titles by major Irish literary figures. From 1909 to 1912, Maunsel was involved in an extended dispute with JJ over the publication of [Dubliners](/works/W_DUBLINERS/), but ultimately backed out of the agreement due to fears of prosecution for obscenity or libel. Hone himself became well-known as a reviewer and later as a biographer. **William Brockman**
