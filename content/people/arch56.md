Scottish writer, theatre critic, and English translator of Henrik Ibsen’s plays. In 1900 upon the publication of eighteen-year-old Joyce’s review of Ibsen’s When We Dead Awaken in the Fortnightly Review, Archer corresponded with Joyce to convey Ibsen’s praise of and gratitude for the review. **Dirk Van Hulle** and **William Brockman**
