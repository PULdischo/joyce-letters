English journalist, correspondent in Geneva for the Daily News during WWI. Following JJ’s altercation with Henry Carr in 1918 over a dispute involving the English Players, Segrue facilitated the Players’ performance in Geneva through a visit to the British consulate there. Segrue died in a German prison camp after having been captured while covering the war in Yugoslavia for the News Chronicle. **William Brockman**
