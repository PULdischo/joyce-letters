Italian novelist, literary critic, translator, and co-founder of the Italian literary magazine Il Convegno. A 1913 trip to Ireland led to Linati’s interest in Irish writers. His Italian translations of [Yeats](/people/yeat65/) and Synge caught the attention of JJ, who in 1918 invited him to translate [A Portrait of the Artist as a Young Man](/works/W_APORTRAIT/). Although this never materialized, his translation of Exiles appeared in three numbers of Il Convegno in 1920 and of the story “Araby” in that magazine in 1924. In September of 1920, JJ sent him a “schema” outlining elements of Ulysses. In 1926 the same magazine published an excerpt from Ulysses translated by Linati. Linati’s translation of Stephen Hero appeared in 1950.**William Brockman**
