Wife of JJ’s friend [John Francis Byrne](/people/byrn80/). Mary Alice Headen was born in Ballina, Co. Mayo, the daughter of Schools Inspector William P. Headen and Mary Paddle. She attended the Dominican Convent in Eccles Street, Dublin before entering Loreto College, Stephen’s Green in 1901 (BA in 1906 at University College). She married Byrne in 1916 and joined him in New York, where he worked as a journalist; their daughter was born the following year. In 1921 she became a naturalised American citizen. They migrated to California, where she filed for divorce in 1957; she died there four years later. **John Simpson**
