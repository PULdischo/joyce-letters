A long-time friend of both JJ and his father, who spent time with both of them walking and socializing throughout Dublin. As an assistant to the sub-sheriff of Dublin, Bergan was in a position to be familiar with many people throughout the city and was known for his wit and playfulness. Following [John Joyce](/people/joyc49/)’s death in December 1931, Bergan, as executor, corresponded frequently with JJ during 1932 to make burial arrangements. Bergan is one of few characters taken from historical Dublin to appear in Ulysses under his own name, featuring prominently in the “Cyclops” episode as “Little Alf”. He was an important source for [Herbert Gorman](/people/gorm93/)’s biography of JJ. **William Brockman**
