Son of JJ’s daughter-in-law [Helen](/people/joyc94/) (née Kastor) by her first marriage to publisher [Leon Samuel Fleischman](/people/flei89/). David Fleischman was born in New York and was educated at Phillips Academy, Andover, Massachusetts from 1934 before studying for a year at Harvard (1940-1), specialising in Literature and History and active in student politics. In 1942 he married Dorothy Rachel Myers, moved to Newton, Massachusetts, and enlisted in the US Army, serving as a Staff Sergeant in France. He subsequently trained as an accountant in Newton and continued there as a CPA until his death in 1967, aged forty-eight. **John Simpson**
