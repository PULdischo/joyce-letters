Rhodes Scholar, Harvard graduate, and partner in the law firm Quinn, Keiffer and Woodward. Kieffer was the uncle of Helen Kieffer (1905-1958), the young woman who became [Lucia Joyce](/people/joyc07/)’s friend and dance companion in Paris and Deauville in 1922 and 1923. Though he was the successor of [John Quinn](/people/quin70/), who had defended The Little Review’s publication of Ulysses, when asked by JJ and [Sylvia Beach](/people/beac87/) to represent JJ in legal action against [Samuel Roth](/people/roth93/)’s unauthorized publication of Ulysses in 1926, Kieffer cabled a curt refusal: “Regret unable to take case.” **William Brockman**
