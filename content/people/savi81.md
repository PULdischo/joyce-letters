Russian-born translator and writer. Living in Paris during and after the WWI years and writing for several literary reviews, she met [Ezra Pound](/people/poun85/), who convinced her to translate into French JJ’s [A Portrait of the Artist as a Young Man](/works/W_APORTRAIT/). In 1920, she and her husband Marcel Bloch offered their flat in Paris to the newly-arrived JJ and his family for several months and, through French poet André Spire, hosted a soirée for JJ to be introduced to the Parisian literary world. Despite some antagonism between author and translator, Dedalus: Portrait de l'artiste jeune par lui-même (Paris: Éditions de la Sirène) was published in 1924; it remained for decades the work’s standard French translation. In 1948, her translation of JJ’s Stephen le Héros (Paris: Gallimard) appeared. She subsequently translated into French works by John Rodker, Virginia Woolf, Christopher Isherwood, and Ivy Compton-Burnett. **William Brockman**
