German singer and translator. Mettal lived in Zurich during and after WWI. She translated JJ’s [Exiles](/works/W_EXILES/) as Verbannte (Zurich: Rascher, 1919). While living in Zurich, she gave several poetry recitations at the Tonhalle and the Kaufleuten theaters. In 1925 she wrote to Sylvia Beach proposing that she translate [Ulysses](/works/W_ULYSSES/), but the project never materialized (Andreas Fischer, James Joyce in Zurich: A Guide [Cham: Palgrave Macmillan, 2020]). **William Brockman**
