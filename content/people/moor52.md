Prominent Anglo-Irish Writer. Moore is remembered largely for his novel Esther Waters (1894), his collection of short stories, The Untilled Field (1903; often said to have been an influence on [Dubliners](/works/W_DUBLINERS/)), and his memoir, Hail and Farewell (1911–14). Moore and JJ had a fraught relationship. In his early years JJ was dismissive of Moore’s writing, but he knew Moore’s work well and at one point while living in Trieste began an Italian translation of some of Moore’s stories. Moore in his turn was critical, at least privately, of Joyce’s writing. Despite the occasional mutual antagonism, their meeting in Paris in 1929 established a friendly relationship. JJ had a floral bouquet sent to Moore’s funeral and complained bitterly in letters to several friends at the newspapers’ failure to take notice of it. **William Brockman**
