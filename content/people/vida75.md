Triestine lawyer. Vidacovich took English lessons from JJ in Trieste, and was involved in JJ’s establishment of the Volta Cinematograph in Dublin in 1909. The two collaborated in translating into Italian Synge’s Riders to the Sea (published in 1929 as “La cavalcata al mare”, Solaria 4, no. 9-10 [September-October 1929], 3-16) and at least part of Yeats’s The Countess Cathleen. **William Brockman**
