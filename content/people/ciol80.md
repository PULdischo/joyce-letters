Parisian journalist. Active in the 1910s and 1920s, Ciolkowska contributed to The Egoist, where her columns entitled “Passing Paris” and “Fighting Paris” appeared in most issues. In 1915 she unsuccessfully attempted to find a publisher in Paris for [A Portrait of the Artist as a Young Man](/works/W_APORTRAIT/). **William Brockman**
