Triestine journalist, librettist, novelist and translator. He met JJ in 1907 and wrote several articles on him and his works for the Italian-language press, including Trieste’s first review of [Ulysses](/works/W_ULYSSES/): “L'Ulisse di James Joyce”, La Nazione 5, no. 78 (1922): 1. **William Brockman**
