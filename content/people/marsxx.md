American publisher. A native Canadian, Marshall had a brief publishing career in New York, first in partnership with Laurence J. Gomme in 1915, and then on his own. In 1916 he published Alfred Kreymborg’s Mushrooms: A Book of Free Forms, and two issues of Others: A Magazine of the Free Verse. In March 1916 Ezra Pound interested Marshall in publishing [A Portrait of the Artist as a Young Man](/works/W_APORTRAIT/). Marshall sent JJ an advance of £10, but in July suddenly left New York for Quebec. **William Brockman**
