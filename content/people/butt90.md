English writer. She and [John Rodker](/people/rodk94/) were married in 1918 and in 1919 founded the Ovid Press, which published limited editions of works by [Pound](/people/poun85/), [Eliot](/people/elio88/), [Lewis](/people/lewi82/), and other modernists. They separated in 1920 when she became involved with Scottish writer and critic Cecil Maitland. Butts became a prolific and wide-ranging author. **William Brockman**
