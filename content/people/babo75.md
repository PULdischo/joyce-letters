French publisher of the series “Les Artistes du livre” (1928–1933) and other titles on art and book design. With [Jack Kahane](/people/kaha87/), he published JJ’s Haveth Childers Everywhere (1930), distributed in the United States by Elbridge Adams’s Fountain Press. **William Brockman**
