American publisher, co-founder of the firm Boni and Liveright, which included two of JJ’s poems in the _Anthology of Irish Verse_ (1922). Liveright negotiated with JJ to take over publication of A Portrait of the Artist as a Young Man and to collaborate in publication of Ulysses, but neither scheme produced results. **William Brockman**
