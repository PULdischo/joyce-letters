Zurich bank employee and friend of JJ. Born in Thessaloniki (then Saloniki), his family moved to Zurich in 1916, where in 1923 he married [Berta Friedli](/people/frie03/) (1903-?). Their son Alfred was born in 1924. A member of the “Club des Étrangers”, the informal group of ex-patriots whom JJ befriended in Zurich in 1916, he spoke with and sometimes corresponded with JJ in Italian, Greek, and French. He helped the Joyce family to find a flat at Seefeldstrasse 73 in 1917. When JJ sang an accompaniment for the English Players’ production of Robert Browning’s In a Balcony, Ruggiero accompanied him on a guitar (which he later maintained that JJ had given him, and which in 1966 Ruggiero donated to the Joyce Tower Museum in Sandycove). The two corresponded occasionally while JJ lived in Paris, and their friendship proved to be critically important during the last months of 1940, when Ruggiero was, with [Carola Giedion-Welcker](/people/gied93/), a key figure in enabling the Joyce family to enter Switzerland during the German occupation of France. **William Brockman**
