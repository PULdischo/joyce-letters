American novelist, best known for his Jurgen: A Comedy of Justice (New York: Robert M. McBride, 1919), whose eponymous character’s racy adventures brought the book to the attention of the New York Society for the Suppression of Vice and its secretary, [John S. Sumner](/people/sumn76/). Banned, the book became notoriously famous and, after being acquitted in its 1922 obscenity trial, became a best-seller. **William Brockman**
