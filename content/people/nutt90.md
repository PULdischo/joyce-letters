American artist. He and his wife Helen lived in Paris from 1921 to 1929. The physician Joseph Collins arranged a meeting between Nutting and Joyce which led to a close friendship between Nutting and his wife with JJ and [Nora Joyce](/people/barn84/). The couples socialized frequently during the Nuttings’ stay in the city from 1921-1929, and the friendship continued into the 1930s after the Nuttings had returned to the United States. Joyce consulted with Nutting to determine the precise color blue to be used for the cover of the first edition of Ulysses. He painted portraits of JJ, Nora Joyce, and [Lucia Joyce](/people/joyc07/) and made a number of sketches of family members. **Geert Lernout** and **William Brockman**
