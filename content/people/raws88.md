English actor. Born in London, Capel Tristan Rawson’s first career was in opera during four years in Cologne. The First World War took him to Zurich, where he met JJ and acted in over a dozen performances of the English Players in 1918 and 1919. He moved to London after the war, where he enjoyed a substantial acting career through the 1940s. **William Brockman**
