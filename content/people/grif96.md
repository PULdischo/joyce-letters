Youngest sister of JJ’s wife [Nora Barnacle](/people/barn84/) and closest to the Joyces. Kathleen (née Catherine) was born, like Nora, in Galway city workhouse. She attended the Convent of Mercy, and worked as a bookbinder and paper-packer. Beautiful, but with a stormy temperament, she had a disastrous affair with a married man and in 1937 (aged forty-two) married John Griffin, then a stevedore, of Middle Street, Galway. She apparently dressed as if pregnant and then (“the laughing stock of Galway”) left with her husband for London, where he followed the family trade of French-polisher. She died there in 1963. **John Simpson**
