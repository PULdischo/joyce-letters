Swiss painter, illustrator and satirical cartoonist. Born Georges-Charles Augsburger in Yverdon-les-Bains in French Switzerland, Augsbourg visited JJ in Saint-Gérard-le-Puy in November 1940 and offered to help getting [Lucia](/people/joyc07/) into Switzerland. Augsbourg exhibited at the progressive Salon des Surindépendants in 1929 but is best known today for his collaboration with Jean Cocteau on La vie de Darius Milhaud en images (1935). For many years he edited and contributed to the Journal de la Maison Charles Veillon. **Ronan Crowley**
