American Endocrinologist. One of the founders of the New York Endocrinological Society, he was the author of numerous books and articles on the endocrine glands, nutrition, and diet. He became well-known for promoting the idea that humanity as a whole could be improved through hormone replacement therapy. **William Brockman**
