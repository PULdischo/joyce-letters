English editor and journalist. Courtney attended Oxford and was elected to a fellowship there. Beginning in the 1880s, he began writing for the British press, and in 1890 left Oxford to become chief dramatic and literary editor for the Daily Telegraph, where he stayed until 1925. He was also editor of the Fortnightly Review from 1894 to 1928. While in that capacity, he accepted JJ’s first publication, “Ibsen’s New Drama” (67, no. 400 [1 April 1900]: 575-90). JJ visited Courtney in London the following month. **William Brockman**
