Swiss architectural historian. Born in Prague, he attended the University of Zurich and earned a doctorate in art history from the University of Munich. He taught at the University of Zurich and at Harvard University and, later, at ETH in Zurich. With his wife, [Carola Giedion-Welcker](/people/gied93/), he befriended James and [Nora Joyce](/people/barn84/) in the 1930s and was instrumental in JJ’s consultation of the ophthalmologist [Vogt](/people/vogt79/) and in enabling the Joyce family’s return to Zurich in 1940. A prolific author and lecturer, his most famous book was Space, Time and Architecture: the Growth of a New Tradition (1941). He died suddenly in Zurich in April 1968. **William Brockman**
