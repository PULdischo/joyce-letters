French critic and writer. Jaloux was an early advocate for Joyce among the French reading public, publishing several positive reviews of his works and calling him – along with Proust and Thomas Mann – one of the “three giants” of the European novel. Joyce wrote to [Harriet Shaw Weaver](/people/weav76/) on 22 October 1922, “I shall write to him [Jaloux]. I dined with him the night before I left Paris and found him very friendly” (LI 188). In 1936, Jaloux became a member of the Académie française. **Dirk Van Hulle** and **William Brockman**
