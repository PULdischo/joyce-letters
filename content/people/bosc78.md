Belgian artist and writer. During WWI he became associated with the Imagists and met [Ezra Pound](/people/poun85/), who helped to place his poems and drawings in The Little Review. Pound reported to JJ that Boschère expressed great praise for [A Portrait of the Artist as a Young Man](/works/W_APORTRAIT/). He visited JJ in Paris in June 1921 and was one of the signers of the 2 February 1927 letter of protest against [Samuel Roth](/people/roth93/)’s publication of [Ulysses](/works/W_ULYSSES/). **William Brockman**
