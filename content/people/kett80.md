Irish parliamentarian, economist, journalist, and soldier, killed in battle during the first World War. He and JJ knew each other while at University College. Kettle published a mixed review of Chamber Music ([1 June 1907], 5). JJ incorporated some elements of Kettle into the character of Robert Hand in Exiles. **William Brockman**
