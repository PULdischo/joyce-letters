Celebrated French painter, printmaker and designer. Matisse was commissioned to design illustrations for the edition of Ulysses published by the Limited Editions Club in 1935. Matisse paid scant attention to Joyce’s books, but instead designed figures from Homer’s Odyssey. **William Brockman**
