Publisher, born in England, but made his name in Paris. With [Henri Babou](/people/babo75/), he published JJ’s Haveth Childers Everywhere (1930), distributed in the United States by Elbridge Adams’s Fountain Press. The following year Kahane established the Obelisk Press, which in 1932 published a limited edition of Pomes Penyeach decorated with initial letters designed by [Lucia Joyce](/people/joyc07/). The Obelisk Press became widely known for its avant-garde list. **William Brockman**
