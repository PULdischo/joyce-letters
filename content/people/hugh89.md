Actor and the second wife of composer [Herbert Hughes](/people/hugh82/). The daughter of Dublin shoemaker James and Maria McKernan, Susan McKernan lived in Drumcondra near the Joyces. As a young woman she moved to London. She attended the Academy of Dramatic Art and, in 1921, joined the Abbey Theatre breakaway company, The Irish Players, for the American tour of [Lennox Robinson](/people/robi86/)’s The White-Headed Boy. She married Hughes in New Jersey the following year. After Herbert’s death in 1937 Suzanne remained in London, dying in Chelsea in December 1972. **John Simpson**
