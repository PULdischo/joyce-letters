Italian journalist from the Trieste area who spent the WWI years in [Zurich](/places/G_CHE_ZURI/). During that time he had an office at Bahnhofplatz and was a correspondent for a newspaper in Turin. When JJ was suffering from vision problems in February and March 1917, he dictated several letters to Battara. Battara drew on his wartime experience in Switzerland to write La Svizzera d'ieri e d'oggi (Milan: R. Caddeo, 1921). **William Brockman**
