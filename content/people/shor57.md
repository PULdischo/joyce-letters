English journalist, reviewer, and editor. His weekly column, “A Literary Letter”, surveyed recent publications from a conservative point of view. In it he judged [Ulysses](/works/W_ULYSSES/) to be “the maddest, muddiest, most loathsome book issued in our own or any other time” (The Sphere 90, no. 1172 [8 July 1922]: 50). **William Brockman**
