French journalist and government official. In preparation for the “séance” to promote Ulysses on 7 December 1921, the then young Benoist-Méchin was engaged to translate into French the end of “Penelope”. He later became a journalist and editor and, in the late 1930s and during WWII, an avid collaborationist, active in the Vichy government. Benoist-Méchin was the source for JJ’s purported quotation that he “put in so many enigmas and puzzles that it will keep the professors busy for centuries” (JJ 521), but the accuracy of his recollection has been disputed. **William Brockman**
