English literary editor and critic. Murray was an early champion of Ulysses, with a strong review of the book in The Nation & the Athenaeum and a longer piece in The Adelphi. He was married to Katherine Mansfield until her death in 1923. **Dirk Van Hulle** and **William Brockman**
