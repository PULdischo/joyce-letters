A correspondent for a New York newspaper, one of JJ’s several Greek friends in Zurich during the WWI years. When JJ was suffering from severe eye problems in 1919, he dictated some letters to Chalas, and later suggested to the American publisher [B. W. Huebsch](/people/hueb76/) (LI 124) that he issue a translation of a pamphlet by Chalas (Le Président Woodrow Wilson: personnification des plus hautes idées politiques de tout temps et les plus réalistes [Zurich: Borsig, 1918]). **William Brockman**
