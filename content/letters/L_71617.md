editorialNotes:
- JJ’s postcard of that date (clearly dated and postmarked) begins “A cable came today
  from Mr. Quinn…” and goes on to write “I have therefore telegraphed to you…” indicating
  that the arrival of the Quinn cable, the writing of the letter, and the sending
  of the telegraph were all on the same day. The transcription (all that is available)
  of the telegram is dated as received 21 March, but this date was probably the result
  of wartime delays.

COPY

(TELEGRAM)

Kensington,

Young St.

21 Mr

17

Zurich 10

Handed Received

in at 6.30 P. M. here at 8.10 A. M.

To [Ezra Pound](/people/poun85/) [5 Holland Place](/places/G_GBR_LOND_06/)

Chambers [London](/places/G_GBR_LOND/) W C

Cable [Quinn](/people/quin70/) my acceptance offer[^1] money to be sent you.

I remit ten shillings cable expenses.

[Joyce](/people/joyc82/)

---

[^1]: JJ here asks Pound to cable John Quinn in New York to accept Quinn’s offer to JJ of £20 for the hand-corrected pages that [B.W. Huebsch](/people/hueb76/) had used as setting copy for the American edition of [A Portrait of the Artist as a Young Man](/works/W_APORTRAIT/). For a full account of this transaction, [JJ to Pound, 19 March 1917](/letters/L_68348/), notes.
